# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be kind. We work with sensitive data for vulnerable people; patience and
  respect are the default.
- Assume good faith.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.

Incidents may be reported to **bbazungu@gmail.com**. Reports are confidential.
